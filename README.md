# Hotelier

## Description
Hotelier is a comprehensive hotel management system designed to streamline and enhance various hotel operations. It offers a user-friendly interface and a robust set of tools for managing reservations, customer relationships, services, invoices, reports, and employee data.

## Key Management Functions
### Reservations and Booking Management
- User-friendly interface for searching and booking rooms
- Dynamic pricing calculation
- Integration with payment gateways for secure transactions

### Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Detailed customer profiles
- Contact information, booking history, preferences
- Communication logs

### Service and Restaurant Management
- Centralized database for hotel services and restaurant management
- Descriptions, pricing, schedules, and availability management

### Invoice Generation
- Automatic detailed invoice creation upon booking confirmation
- Breakdown of charges including room rates, services, and taxes

### Reporting and Analytics
- Data gathering for occupancy rates, revenue, customer trends, and operational performance
- Insight generation for data-driven decision-making

### Employee Management
- Secure storage of employee information
- Work schedule tracking
- Integration with payroll systems for HR management

### Usage
- Reservations and Booking: Navigate to the booking section, search for available rooms, select dates, and complete the reservation process.
- Customer Management: Access customer profiles, update information, and review booking history.
- Service Management: Manage service and restaurant offerings, update descriptions, pricing, and schedules.
- Invoice Management: View and generate detailed invoices for bookings.
- Reports and Analytics: Generate and review reports on occupancy rates, revenue, and other key metrics.
- Employee Management: Manage employee data, track work schedules, and integrate with payroll systems.
